A private photo diary

Your life, one photo at a time.

Photonnic is a quiet place to keep one photo for each day, add a few words when you want, and come back to the moments that matter.

Open Photonnic How your photos stay private

A diary you can see.

No streaks to protect, no feed to scroll, no likes to count. Just a calendar that fills, square by square, with the days you lived. Miss a day and nothing breaks: it simply waits for you.

How it works

  1. 1

    Capture

    Tap Capture and take a photo with your phone’s own camera, or choose one you already took. A photo from another day can go to that day.

  2. 2

    Add a few words

    A caption is optional. One line, a few lines, or none at all.

  3. 3

    Watch the month fill

    Every photo takes its day’s square. Missed a day? Tap it later and fill it from your library.

  4. 4

    Look back

    See the same day a month or a year ago, and your very first photo.

Private by design

Your photos stay on your phone

Photos and captions are saved in Photonnic’s own storage on your device. They are never uploaded: there is no Photonnic server that holds them.

No account

Nothing to sign up for. No email address, no password, no profile.

Location is your choice

Photonnic keeps the location a photo already carries. Adding your current location to new camera photos is off unless you turn it on. Locations stay on your phone too.

Reminders, minimally

If you turn on the daily reminder, your browser gives the Photonnic reminder server an anonymous delivery address, with your reminder time and time zone. If you also choose “skip once today’s photo is in”, it learns the date of your latest photo: never the photo. Turning reminders off deletes this.

No tracking

Neither this page nor the app uses analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Your backups, your place

An exported backup is a file you control. Photonnic never sees where you keep it.

Install it like an app

iPhone and iPad: open Photonnic in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open it in Chrome and tap Install app.

After the first visit Photonnic opens, and saves photos, without a connection.

Backups you can hold

Export all photos makes a single .zip on your phone, with every photo at the quality you saved it, and its caption, date and location.

Restore reads it back, on the same phone or a new one, and asks before replacing any day that already has a photo. A broken or unfamiliar file is refused before anything changes.

Your browser can clear its storage, so export now and then.

A nudge, if you want one

Turn on a daily reminder in Settings and choose its time. It is optional and needs notification permission.

On iPhone it works from the Home Screen on iOS 16.4 or later. Delivery depends on your phone, its browser and its focus settings.
