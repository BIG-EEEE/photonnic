Photonic

Navigable prototype. Today is Thursday 9 October 2026 and no photo has been taken yet.

  1. Tap any photo to open it. Swipe it, use the arrows or the strip
  2. Back returns the photo to its square
  3. Tap Capture (the header or the middle tab)
  4. Continue, then Allow camera access
  5. Press the shutter, or the library thumbnail
  6. Add a caption, then Save to 9 Oct
  7. The photo lands in today's square
  8. Capture again to see the replace flow
  9. Tap a past empty day (6 Oct) to fill it from the library
  10. More, then Settings
Sample photos: Unsplash via picsum.photos. Settings links and Export are shown but do not leave the prototype.
